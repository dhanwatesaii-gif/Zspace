Transform passive space textbooks into an interactive leadership experience where students command a crew on the Moon (Artemis Base Alpha) or Mars (Olympus Station) using real NASA science.

PROBLEM STATEMENT • SLIDE 02 / 12

⚠️ The 3 Core Problems We Solve

PRESENTER 1 • PROBLEM

1. Passive STEM Learning

Space science is often taught through static diagrams and raw telemetry tables. Students rarely experience cause-and-effect decision-making during a solar storm or life-support emergency.

2. Hardware & Size Barriers

Traditional 3D space simulators require expensive gaming PCs and multi-gigabyte downloads, locking out school laptops and web-first classrooms.

3. Missing Human Teamwork

Most space games focus only on piloting mechanics rather than the human, biological, and psychological trade-offs of keeping a 4-person crew alive and united.

🎙️ Speaker Note: "We tackled three major gaps: passive textbook learning, heavy hardware barriers in traditional 3D simulators, and the lack of human teamwork and resource decision-making in space games."

Captures live WebGL frames, automatically stamps Commander callsign, mission phase, location, and progress %, and builds a downloadable photo portfolio.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE • SLIDE 05 / 12

🏗️ Dual-Layer Architecture & 10-Mode State Machine

PRESENTER 2 • ARCHITECTURE

Master State Controller (App.tsx)

  • Manages 10 seamless ViewMode transitions: WALK → ELEVATOR_RIDE → COCKPIT → COUNTDOWN → IN_FLIGHT → SPACE_ORBIT → WARP_DRIVE → PLANET_DESCENT → PLANET_SURFACE → CONVERSATION.
  • Tracks real-time Outpost Survival Stats: Power, Oxygen, Water, Morale.

3D Viewport + 2D Cybernetic HUD

  • Scene.tsx: 60 FPS React Three Fiber canvas with preserveDrawingBuffer: true for instant photo capture.
  • Overlay.tsx: Responsive HUD, Kid-Friendly Science Glossary (G), 4-Way Dialogue Wheel, and Photo Gallery Modal.
🎙️ Speaker Note: "Our architecture separates the 60 FPS 3D WebGL scene from the reactive 2D Cybernetic HUD, coordinated by a central 10-state machine in App.tsx."

IMPLEMENTATION PART 1 • SLIDE 07 / 12

🪐 8-Planet Solar System & Planetary Descent

PRESENTER 3 • 3D WORLD

Interactive Solar System (SpaceOrbitNav.tsx)

  • Renders the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth (+ orbiting Moon), Mars, Asteroid Belt (120 instanced rocks), Jupiter, Saturn (with rings), Uranus, and Neptune.
  • Clicking any planet smoothly lerps the camera and displays NASA telemetry (diameter, moons, temperature, fun fact).

Touchdown & Sunrise Lighting (PlanetarySurface.tsx)

  • HemisphereLight Bounce eliminates pitch-black shadows at low sun angles.
  • Moon Descent: Blue-white vacuum retro-plumes & grey crater dust spray.
  • Mars Descent: Orange plasma entry glow, swirling red dust storm & shockwave ring.
🎙️ Speaker Note: "In our 3D world implementation, players can inspect all 8 planets plus the Moon in orbit, then watch a custom descent animation tailored to either the Moon's vacuum or Mars's dusty atmosphere."
